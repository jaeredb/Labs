# Request for Proposal: Installation of Wireless Infrastructure

## About Us
JBcompany is a growing organization that needs a strong and reliable wireless connection for our network. This is important because it helps improve connectivity and supports our staff with fast and stable Wi-Fi across the office.

Our mission is to provide easy-to-use technology that helps people work better every day. The services we use must be designed with the customer’s needs in mind.

---

## Scope of Project

### Objectives
- Create a wireless network that is secure and always on.
- Ensure the entire office has Wi-Fi with no dead spots.
- Make sure the Wi-Fi works smoothly with our existing wired network.
- Provide simple tools that let us check the network, monitor performance, and fix small issues.
- Have the vendor continue supporting us even after the installation.

### Scope of Work
- Perform a site check to determine optimal access point placement.
- Install and configure all wireless access points.
- Connect each access point to the wired network.
- Turn on security settings, including WPA3, and set up a secure guest network.
- Test the network for speed, coverage, and signal strength in all areas.
- Provide documents, diagrams, and instructions explaining the whole system.
- Provide post-installation support, updates, and maintenance.

### Deliverables
- A complete wireless site survey and coverage map.
- A full list of all equipment and materials used.
- A setup and configuration report.
- Network diagrams that show where each access point is placed.
- A short training session for our staff on how to monitor and manage the network.
- A support and maintenance plan for post-installation assistance.

---

## Technical Requirements
- Support for Wi-Fi 6 or newer.
- Full coverage with no weak or dead zones.
- Security features such as WPA3, segmentation, and a secure guest network.
- A dashboard for monitoring access points, traffic, and alerts.
- Easy future expandability.

---

## Vendor Qualifications
- Hands-on experience with wireless network installation.
- Examples of similar completed projects.
- At least three references from past clients.
- Network-related certifications are an advantage.

---

## Evaluation Criteria
- Total project cost, including hardware, installation, and annual maintenance.
- Technical design and installation approach.
- Project timeline with key steps and expected completion date.
- Vendor reputation, references, and quality of past work.
